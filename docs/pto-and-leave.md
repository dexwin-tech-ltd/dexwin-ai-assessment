# PTO and leave

Northline's time-off program is administered in Workday. Managers should
encourage planning so coverage stays intact across pods and regions during
peak shipping weeks and inventory freezes.

Full-time employees accrue **20 days of PTO per calendar year** (1.67 days each month).
Part-time employees scheduled under 30 hours/week receive a pro-rated bank.
Unused PTO may carry over a maximum of **5 days** into the next calendar year;
anything above that is forfeited on 1 January.

Request leave in Workday at least **14 days** in advance for planned time off.
Sick leave is a separate bank: **8 days** per year and does not come out of PTO.
Parental leave is 12 weeks paid for eligible employees after 12 months of service.
There is no unlimited-PTO policy.
