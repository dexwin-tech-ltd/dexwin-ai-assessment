# On-call

The Platform team runs a weekly primary on-call rotation. Secondary cover is
the previous primary. Handoff is Monday 10:00 in the employee's home timezone.

After-hours **Severity 1** pages go to PagerDuty schedule `#platform-oncall`.
If the primary does not acknowledge within **15 minutes**, escalate to Engineering
Manager **Dana Okonkwo**.

Comp time: primary on-call earns **1 extra PTO day** for each full week of primary
duty, logged in Workday by the manager. Do not swap weeks in a spreadsheet —
update the PagerDuty schedule so paging still works.

Sev2 and below can wait for business hours unless a customer-facing SLO is
already burning error budget.
