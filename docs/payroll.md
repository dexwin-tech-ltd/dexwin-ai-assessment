# Payroll

Northline pays **biweekly on Fridays** by direct deposit only. Paper checks are
not issued.

Payroll cutoff is **Monday 12:00** local time in the week of payday. Changes
after cutoff (new bank account, bonus, missed hours) land on the following cycle.

Tax, name, and address changes go in Workday. Questions about a specific
paycheck: **payroll@northline.example** — do not CC customers or vendors.

Equity events and bonuses are on a separate calendar published by Finance.
Compensation bands and individual pay are not published in this knowledge base.
