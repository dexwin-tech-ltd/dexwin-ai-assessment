# Office access

Headquarters is **400 Market Street, Floor 7**. Bring photo ID on your first
day. Front Desk issues a badge; guests never borrow an employee's badge.

Register visitors in **Envoy at least 24 hours** ahead. After **19:00**, the
lobby requires badge plus PIN. Do not prop stairwell doors.

If you forget your badge, Front Desk can issue a day pass after matching your
photo in Workday. Lost badges must be deactivated the same day via #it-help.

Contractors follow the same badge rules. Cameras in the office are limited to
lobby and loading dock.
