# Travel

Book work travel in **Navan**. Do not book on a consumer site and expense it
unless Navan cannot price the route — then attach a screenshot of the Navan miss.

Flights under **6 hours**: economy. Flights of **6 hours or more**: premium
economy is allowed. First class requires **VP approval** in writing before
booking.

International trips need a travel-advisory check in Navan. Safety holds from
Security override a manager's "we still need you there."
Rental cars need insurance via Navan; do not decline the corporate coverage.

Personal travel mixed onto a work trip is allowed if the company cost does not
increase. Document the comparison fare.
