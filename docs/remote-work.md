# Remote work

Northline is hybrid. The office is for collaboration, not a daily attendance
contest, but there is a default pattern.

Default in-office days are **Tuesday and Thursday** (2 days per week) for
employees within a commutable distance of an office.
Core hours are **10:00–16:00** in the employee's home timezone.

International remote work of more than **10 days per year** needs approval from
People and Legal before travel. Working from a country not on the approved
list can create payroll and data-residency issues — do not "just try it."

Fully remote roles are listed as such on the offer letter. Do not assume a
hybrid role can convert to fully remote without a People ticket.
