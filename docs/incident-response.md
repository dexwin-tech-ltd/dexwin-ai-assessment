# Incident response

Northline runs a blameless incident process. The goal is restore service, then
learn — not to find someone to blame on the Slack thread.

Severity 1: page on-call and name an **incident commander**. Post in `#incidents`
and update the public status page at least every **30 minutes** until resolved.
Severity 2: incident commander optional; still file a ticket before the next
business day.

Customer-facing wording goes through the **Support lead** only. Engineers should
not email customers directly from personal inboxes during an incident.

A blameless postmortem is due within **5 business days** of mitigate. Action
items need an owner and a date. Repeat Sev1 in 30 days triggers a review with
the VP of Engineering.
