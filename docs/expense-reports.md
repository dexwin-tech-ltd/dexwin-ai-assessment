# Expense reports

Northline reimburses reasonable business costs. Do not wait until quarter-end
to clean up a pile of receipts — Finance closes the books on a fixed calendar.

Submit expense reports in **Concur within 14 days** of the trip or purchase date.
Itemized receipts are required for any line **over $25**.
Manager approval covers reports totaling under **$500**. Amounts of $500 or more
also need Finance approval.

Use a personal card only when a corporate card is unavailable; reimbursement
lands on the **next payroll** after approval. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless
pre-approved for a customer dinner. Mileage uses the current IRS rate and must
include origin, destination, and business purpose.

Never submit expenses on behalf of another employee.
